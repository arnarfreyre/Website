# Clinical Pharmacology Exam Platform

## LYF516G - Klínísk Aðgengisfræði

An interactive web-based examination platform for clinical pharmacology studies, featuring comprehensive patient case analysis and multi-mode learning experiences.

## Features

### 📚 Study Mode
- Practice questions with helpful hints
- Immediate feedback and guidance
- No time restrictions
- Learn at your own pace

### ⏱️ Exam Mode
- Full 180-minute timed examination
- Realistic exam conditions
- Automatic submission on timeout
- Professional exam environment

### 📊 Review Mode
- Comprehensive answer review
- Detailed scoring breakdown
- Performance analysis
- Learning insights

## Exam Content

### Patient Case Study
The exam centers around a detailed clinical case:
- **Patient**: 68-year-old male (KS)
- **Conditions**: Heart failure, atrial fibrillation, diabetes, kidney dysfunction
- **Comprehensive Data**: Medical history, medications, vital signs, lab results, physical examination

### Question Categories
1. **Pharmacokinetic Calculations** (20 points)
   - Creatinine clearance
   - Drug clearance calculations
   - Volume of distribution

2. **Lab Values & Interpretation** (15 points)
   - Abnormal value identification
   - Clinical significance
   - Corrected values

3. **Drug History & Interactions** (20 points)
   - Medication review
   - Drug interactions
   - Contraindications

4. **Acute Treatment Plan** (25 points)
   - Emergency interventions
   - Medication adjustments
   - Safety measures

5. **Long-term Management** (20 points)
   - Dose optimization
   - Monitoring plans
   - Lifestyle modifications

6. **Follow-up & Monitoring** (10 points)
   - Lab monitoring schedules
   - Patient education
   - Warning signs

7. **Bonus: Digoxin Calculations** (10 points)
   - Loading dose
   - Maintenance dose

## Usage

1. Open `index.html` in a modern web browser
2. Select your preferred mode (Study/Exam/Review)
3. Navigate through the patient case information
4. Answer questions at your own pace (Study mode) or within time limit (Exam mode)
5. Review your performance and learn from the feedback

## Requirements

- Modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- JavaScript enabled
- Screen resolution of 1024x768 or higher recommended

## Language

The exam is presented in **Icelandic** as it's designed for the University of Iceland's clinical pharmacology course.

## Scoring

- **Total Points**: 110 (including 10 bonus points)
- **Passing Score**: 50 points minimum
- **Grading**: Automatic calculation with percentage display

## Educational Purpose

This platform is designed to help medical and pharmacy students:
- Practice clinical decision-making
- Understand drug interactions and contraindications
- Master pharmacokinetic calculations
- Prepare for real clinical scenarios
